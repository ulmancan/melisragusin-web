02Artist Bio

Melis Ragusin is a mixed-media and new media artist working across AI-driven video art, real-time generative systems, fine art prints, and sculptural wall pieces. Her practice explores impermanence, emotional presence, and the shifting nature of identity through cinematic motion and evolving digital structures.

Born in Izmir, Turkey, she studied International Relations & Economics at Towson University (USA) and completed the Artificial Intelligence: Business Strategies and Applications program at UC Berkeley Executive Education. She continues to expand her practice through advanced studies in generative systems and cinematic storytelling.

Ragusin moves fluidly between tactile materials and algorithmic environments, building visual systems that evolve over time. Her work bridges digital aesthetics with physical craftsmanship and has been exhibited internationally.

03Artist Statement

My practice explores environments where visuals evolve through internal logic rather than fixed timelines. I am drawn to impermanence — how identities shift, how atmospheres transform, how emotions surface and dissolve.

Each work functions as a living presence. Some are structured as immersive loops; others operate as generative systems that continuously reshape themselves.

I create to explore presence — and to invite viewers into their own emotional interpretation. The same piece can evoke entirely different experiences from person to person. That variability is central to my practice.

Mixed-Media

Mixed media art combines physical materials on surfaces like MDF panels or canvas. I use elements such as epoxy resin, broken glass, chipboard, and cloth to create layered, tactile works that are both sculptural and pictorial.
