New Media & Mixed-Media Artist

Transformation, metamorphosis, and sarcasm — the art of becoming and unbecoming.

My work dives into the absurd beauty of change — chosen or imposed. Through generative visuals and shifting illusions, I explore identity, rupture, and the space between reality and hallucination.
